How It Works

From complaint to resolution, in four simple steps.

1. RegisterDescribe the problem. Your name, mobile number and ward are filled in.
2. Auto-routeIt goes to the officer for your field and ward.
3. TrackSee which office has it and its time limit.
4. Get ResolvedConfirm the fix, or send it up if it is not fixed.
