What happens next

  1. A field worker accepts your complaint. You will see "Allotted" and "Work in progress".
  2. When the work is done, a code comes to your mobile. Give it to the worker only if the problem is fixed.
  3. If the time limit passes, the officers get a notice and the complaint moves up to the next officer.
